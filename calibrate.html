Calibrate PowerPoint's volume

← Back to Volume Fix

Volume Fix turns clips down by changing PowerPoint's per-clip volume setting. On Office 365 PowerPoint 16 the setting is a plain linear amplitude scale (40% = −8 dB), measured with this page. If you show with a different PowerPoint version, this page measures yours: play a calibration deck, record what PowerPoint sends to your sound card, and drop the recording here. Do this on the PowerPoint version you show with; it only needs doing again after a major Office update.

Record it with loopback

  1. In Audacity, click Audio Setup → Host → Windows WASAPI.
  2. Audio Setup → Recording Device → the device PowerPoint plays through, ending in (loopback). Set Recording Channels to 2 (Stereo).
  3. Close other programs that make sound. Loopback records before the speaker volume, so you can turn the speakers down.
  4. Press Record in Audacity, then open the calibration deck and start the slide show from the first slide (F5). It runs by itself, about 80 seconds.
  5. After the last slide's tone, stop recording and choose File → Export Audio → WAV, 32-bit float.
